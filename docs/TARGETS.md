# Balance and realism targets

These are the real-world numbers the game is tuned against, all on one page. Each section says where the source is, where the game measures it, and whether we tune toward it yet.
- **Run and pass results** are measured by `npm run balance` (`balance.txt`).
- **Route shapes and splits** are checked by `python analysis/compare_game.py`. The full generated tables are in `analysis/out/`.

The current measured values live in the baseline in [PROGRESS.md](PROGRESS.md). This page holds only the targets.

## Run game (FBS)
**Status:** active target.

**Sources:**
- FBS teams averaged 4.35–4.51 yards per rush in 2018–21 (AP; sacks count as rushes, so RB carries alone run higher, about 4.8–5).
- Starting FBS backs average about 3.1 yards after contact per attempt (PFF, 2023).
- The definitions are from Football Study Hall:
  - **stuff rate:** carries ending at or behind the line
  - **opportunity rate:** carries gaining 4+
  - **power success:** 3rd or 4th and 2 or less converted
- The league-wide levels below are from general knowledge; I couldn't confirm them in one current table. Re-check them against CFBD or SP+ data when possible.

These targets are for the harness auto-runner, which has no jukes, so a human should do a bit better:

| Situation | YPC | Median | Stuffs (≤0) | 4+ | 10+ |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Light box (2 Man Under, Cover 1 Robber) | 4.5–5.5 | ~3 | 15–22% | 45–50% | 10–14% |
| Loaded box (Cover 0 Blitz) | 2.5–3.5 | ~1.5 | 28–35% | — | ~5% |

| Other | Target |
| --- | --- |
| Short yardage, 3rd and 1 (`SHORT` rows: Power, Iso, QB Sneak, QB Power) | ~70% converted (FBS power success about 65–72%) |
| Yards after contact (`fallForward`, broken tackles) | ~3 per carry for starting backs |
| Fumbles per carry | Not targeted. Botched exchanges are 1% (`HANDOFF_ODDS`), plus pitch drops |

**Harness columns:**
- `ypc`, `median`, `stuff` (0 or fewer yards), `4+`, `10+`
- `contact`: yards past the LOS at first contact. A negative value means a defender was in the backfield.

## Alignments and splits (NFL)
**Status:** active target. These are applied in `src/data/plays.ts` (`OL_ALIGN`, `WIDE`, `SLOT`, `TRIPS_SLOT`). The source is NFL tracking at the snap (`analysis/out/splits.md`).

**Hashes:** college hashes are 20.0 yd from the sideline, NFL 23.58. Outside-WR sideline distances transfer; slot splits are tied to the ball.

| Player | NFL median | Game |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Guard | 1.55 yd from the ball | 1.5 |
| Tackle | 3.13 yd from the ball | 3.0 |
| Inline TE | 4.9 yd from the ball | 4.8 |
| Outside WR | about 11.8 yd from the sideline (11.5–14 by formation) | 11 at a mid-field ball, 8.2 to the boundary, 13.7 to the field |
| Slot, 2x2 | 7.1 yd from the ball to the boundary, 9.3 to the field | 6 / 10 (8 at a mid-field ball) |
| Trips #2 | about 19.8 yd from the sideline | 9.5 yd from the ball at mid-field |
| Receiver depth | 1.2–1.6 yd off the LOS | 0.6 (on the line) / 1.5 (off) |

The "game alignments" block at the bottom of `analysis/out/splits.md` predates the split changes. Re-run `compare_game.py` to refresh it.

## Concept frequency (NFL)
**Status:** reference for building the playbook. Not a balance target.

Most common same-side pairs (outer/inner), from `analysis/out/concepts.md`:
1. GO/OUT (5.3%)
2. HITCH/HITCH (4.5%)
3. GO/GO (3.8%)
4. GO/POST (3.3%)
5. GO/HITCH (3.3%)
6. IN/CROSS (3.1%)
7. HITCH/GO (2.8%)
8. POST/CROSS (2.8%)
9. SLANT/SLANT (2.5%)
10. HITCH/POST (2.3%)

**Under center (I-Form, Singleback):**
- TE routes are mostly cross, flat and corner.
- WR routes are mostly cross, corner and go.

That's why the play action from I-Form and Ace leans on boots with crossers and corners. College keeps spread, Air Raid, RPO and option concepts even where the NFL data is thin.
