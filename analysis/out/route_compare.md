Generated by `analysis/compare_game.py`. Ours come from `ROUTES` in `src/data/plays.ts`.

- **depth**: break depth (yd past the alignment), or top depth for hitch/curl/comeback.
- **go/seam** have no break: the angle is the overall line from the alignment to the end of the route.
- **hitch/curl/comeback**: the NFL route window ends at the throw, about when they plant, so the leg back isn't measured.
- **leg angle**: direction after the break in degrees from straight downfield; + is inside, beyond ±90 comes back toward the LOS. NFL legs are measured over the 5 yd after the break, so rounded breaks read less sharp than a waypoint corner.
- **flag**: our value is outside the NFL middle 50%.
